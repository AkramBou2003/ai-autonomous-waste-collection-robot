# Mechanical design

The arm and its parts were designed with SolidWorks. The report shows the complete arm, shoulder base, elbow, wrist and gripper, as well as the four-wheel vehicle base.

## Arm parts

1. Shoulder / base
2. Elbow
3. Wrist
4. Gripper

The report identifies an MG995 servo for the shoulder and other servos for the remaining joints. The exact dimensions and servo angles should be checked against the physical assembly.

## Mobile base

The vehicle has four DC motors and carries the arm and the waste container. The chassis must be stable enough to support the arm, electronics, battery and collected object.

The images in `assets/solidworks/` illustrate the mechanical design of the arm and robot vehicle.
